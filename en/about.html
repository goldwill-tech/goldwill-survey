GOLDWILL INDUSTRIAL (HK) LIMITED

A specialist supplier of surveying instrument accessories and cable assemblies, serving surveying professionals. The range covers Leica, Topcon, Sokkia, Trimble, Pentax, Nikon, SOUTH, CHCNAV, Hi-Target, FOIF, GeoMax, BOIF, TJOP, DADI, Stonex, Spectra, Ashtech and NovAtel instruments.

Product lines include instrument batteries, chargers, data and communication cables, antennas and radio accessories, prisms and tribrachs, carbon fibre poles and brackets, and instrument cases - 431 models available in total.

Custom orders are welcome. Consistent quality, a complete range, on-time delivery.
